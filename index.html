Generative Engine Optimization

What GEO is, and what it changes.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of getting a brand surfaced and cited inside AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, rather than only in blue-link search results. Answer engines quote passages, not pages.

How Generative Engine Optimization differs from classic SEO
What changes Classic SEOBlue links GEOAI answers
What you compete for A position in a list A sentence inside the answer
The unit that wins The page The passage
How you measure it Rank, clicks, impressions Citations and share of answer
What earns the trust Links and domain authority Entity clarity and checkable claims
Who has to read you Googlebot, Bingbot GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot
Where they overlap Crawlability, schema, speed and clean structure carry both. Skip them and neither one works.

GEO is not a replacement for technical SEO. It is what technical SEO was already pointing at. Fix the crawl, mark up the entity, answer early, and both surfaces improve on one commit.

This page runs the playbook: schema graph, answer-first FAQ, crawler rules and an llms.txt brief, all live on the URL you are reading. Nobody controls what a model quotes. The work removes every reason an engine would skip you.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and why does it matter?

GEO is the practice of getting your brand surfaced and cited inside AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, rather than only in blue-link results. Answer engines pull from structured, verifiable, entity-rich content, so the work is schema, entity signals, direct answer formatting and crawler configuration.

How is GEO different from SEO?

Classic SEO competes for a position in a list. GEO competes to be the source a model quotes. There is no rank to track, so you measure citations. The unit pulled is a passage, not a page. Entity clarity outweighs link volume. The technical foundations overlap almost completely.

How do you get a brand cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

Answer the question in the first two sentences of a section. Mark the page up so a machine can tell a person from a product from a service. Publish claims a model can check. Allow GPTBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot in robots.txt. Add an llms.txt brief.
