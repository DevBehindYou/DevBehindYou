Privacy

What this page collects.

Last updated: October 2026. This page is run by Ashutosh Sharma (DevBehindYou), based in India. Questions: devbehindyou@gmail.com.

Analytics, only if you allow it

The page asks before it loads Google Analytics 4. If you choose “No thanks”, or make no choice, no analytics script loads and no request goes to Google. If you allow it, Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and records which hand-off links you click (to the portfolio, the request form, or a project or build demo). It does not receive your name or email.

You can change your choice at any time: . Your choice is saved in your own browser (localStorage) under the name dby-consent.

Hosting

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Project requests and email

The “Start a project request” button opens a separate form at devbehindyou-app-github.netlify.app. It asks for your email, phone number and a short project description, which Netlify stores so I can reply. Emails you send to devbehindyou@gmail.com are kept in that inbox.

I use these details only to reply about your project. They are not sold or shared for advertising.

How long, and your rights

  • Project requests and emails are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact, then deleted.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent by emailing devbehindyou@gmail.com. You get an answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 give you these rights and the right to complain to a regulator.

The full portfolio at devbehindyou.com is a separate site with its own policy.

May I count your visit with Google Analytics? It sets cookies, and only loads if you allow it.
